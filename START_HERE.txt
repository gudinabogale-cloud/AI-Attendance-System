🆘 TROUBLESHOOTING:

   Problem: Camera not working?
   Solution: Grant camera permissions in browser

   Problem: Face not detected?
   Solution: Ensure good lighting and look at camera

   Problem: Low accuracy?
   Solution: Capture more images (30-50) and retrain model

   Problem: Module not found error?
   Solution: Run setup.bat to install dependencies
